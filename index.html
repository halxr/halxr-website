The problem

One class. One pace. Every student somewhere else.

A teacher can teach at only one level and one pace. Students who start behind can't follow, and students who are ahead learn less than they could. Teaching to the middle costs learning at both ends.

Starting behind isn't the same as learning slowly. A capable student who begins in the wrong place keeps falling further behind, because every lesson builds on the one they missed.

loop 01Lesson · real time

  1. material
  2. student
  3. evaluation
  4. learner model
  5. next material

Every answer updates what HAL knows about the student, and the next step changes on the spot.

loop 02Course · periodic

  1. learner model
  2. new material
  3. course optimization
  4. this student's curriculum

Over weeks, HAL rebuilds the path through the course around how this student actually learns.

Both loops run for one student. No one else's curriculum changes.

The signal

A record of what you can do. Not just what you say you can.

AI can write any cover letter, and a GPA means something different at every school. HAL sees how students actually learn: how fast they master new material, what they still remember a week later, how they handle hard problems and explain their thinking.

That becomes a capability profile that students own and can choose to share with employers, universities and programs that need a better signal.
