One teacher. Thirty students. Thirty different speeds.

Teach to the slowest student and you hold back the fastest. Teach to the middle and the rest fall behind. Learning is individual, but classrooms can't be.

Meanwhile teachers are scarce and stretched thin, and the old signals of what students can do (grades, essays, cover letters) mean less every year.

The signal

A record of what you can do. Not just what you say you can.

AI can write any cover letter, and a GPA means something different at every school. HAL sees how students actually learn: how they reason, how fast they master new material, how they handle hard problems.

That becomes a capability profile that students own and can choose to share with employers, universities and programs that need a better signal.
