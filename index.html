Personalized learning

Every lesson, built for the person taking it.

Halxr turns any topic into a lesson plan and narrated lessons shaped around each learner: their pace, what they already know and how much jargon they want explained.

Talk to us See how it works

The problem

One-size-fits-all content leaves most learners behind.

Courses and explainer videos are made once, for an imagined “average” learner. Beginners get lost in jargon. Experts sit through basics they already know. People who need more time get rushed.

Human tutors adapt to each learner, but they don't scale. Learners are left choosing between content that doesn't fit them and help they can't access.

How it works

From a learner profile to a lesson made for them

  1. 1

    Tell us about you

    Learners share their goal, what they already know, how fast they want to move and how much technical language they're comfortable with.

  2. 2

    Get a personal plan

    Halxr builds a lesson plan sized to their available time, with objectives and key terms pitched at their level.

  3. 3

    Learn, and it adapts

    Each module becomes a narrated lesson with a glossary and quiz. Quiz results and “explain that” requests shape what comes next.
